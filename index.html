// 01 — about me

A developer who ships end-to-end

van@dev: ~

I’m a full-stack developer based in Douala, Cameroon. I don’t just write features — I build complete products: rich frontends with Angular and Next.js, clean APIs with Python / FastAPI and PHP / Laravel, and cross-platform mobile apps with Flutter.

My daily driver is Linux — the terminal is where I live. I containerize with Docker, provision infrastructure as code with Terraform, deploy to AWS, and understand the networking underneath it all: DNS, TCP/IP, HTTP, SSH.

And my biggest edge: I’ve become excellent at wielding AI — not as a gimmick, but as a genuine engineering multiplier to architect, debug, refactor and ship faster without trading away quality.
